# Layered depth

Use it on pages that two kinds of reader land on: people who want the answer and people who want the detail
behind it. The first group stops reading at the first term they don't know. The second group scrolls past plain words
without minding. So the top layer is for everyone, and the detail goes below it, marked so readers can skip it.

## The layers

1. **The top layer.** What the reader gets or can do, the problem it solves, in the words the reader uses. One
   screen at most.
2. **The detail layer.** The specifics people check before they trust it: names, numbers, formats, limits. Terms
   from the field are fine here because the reader chose to come this far. Give it a heading that says what it holds.
3. **The limits layer.** What this doesn't cover, so the wrong people rule themselves out early.

## An invented example

A library's e-book lending page, before (the facts here are the writer's: five loans at a time, three weeks each,
no late fees, a junior card for children):

> Titles are free to members with a library card, served via the Lendlight platform under a metered-access licence with a concurrent-user cap of one per
> copy; up to five concurrent loans per member, 21-day term. Holds are queued FIFO and DRM-wrapped EPUB or PDF is
> auto-returned at expiry, so no overdue charges apply. Juvenile titles require a junior membership.

After:

> **Borrow e-books free with your library card.**
> You can borrow up to five at a time, for three weeks each. If someone else has the book, you join a waiting list and
> we email you when it's ready. [Check: does the waiting list send an email? The source doesn't say.]
>
> **How it works, in more detail**
> Each e-book copy can be borrowed by one person at a time. Books come in EPUB or PDF and return themselves on the due date, so
> there are no late fees. The lending app is Lendlight.
>
> **What this doesn't cover**
> Children's e-books need a junior card. [Check: audiobooks and magazines are on a separate page? The source doesn't say.]

What changed: the opening says what the reader gets and does; the detail still names the platform and the formats;
the exclusions are stated plainly, and anything the source left out is a bracketed gap for the writer.

## Checks

- Cover the detail layer. Does the top layer still stand alone?
- Does the detail layer contradict or add a condition the top layer left out? If so, move the condition up.
- Is there a limits layer where a wrong guess costs the reader time or money?
