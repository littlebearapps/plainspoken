# Examples

Every business, person and number below is invented. Each draft shows which checklist items fire and why. The
suggested fixes remove or restate; none adds a fact.

## 1. A supplier follow-up email (kind: follow-up, profile pronoun: I)

> Hi Marcus, just following up on my last email in case you missed it. We've got a limited run of the hex bolts and
> they'll be gone by Friday, so I'd move fast. We guarantee they'll fix your rattling problem.

- **R8 Follow-up.** "just following up on my last email in case you missed it" only makes sense after the earlier
  note. Fix: say what the note is about in one line, so it stands alone.
- **R3 Pressure.** "gone by Friday, so I'd move fast" is a deadline the writer hasn't shown is real. Fix: give the
  actual stock level, or drop the line.
- **R2 Outcome promises.** "guarantee they'll fix your rattling problem" promises a result that depends on the
  fitting. Fix: drop the guarantee and say what the bolts are: "[Writer: what these bolts fit, from the order]".
- **R4 Pronoun stance.** "We've got" for one person's stock. Fix: "I have".
- **R9 Unasked advice.** "I'd move fast" tells Marcus what to do. Fix: cut it.

## 2. A first message to a local club (kind: outreach)

> Hello, I'm reaching out to all the clubs in the area about our new scoreboard hire. It's free to look, no
> obligation, and 3 clubs in your league have already signed up.

- **R7 First contact.** "all the clubs in the area" could go to anyone unchanged, and it says nothing about where the
  address came from or how to say no. Fix: one named observation about this club, who is writing, and a plain "no
  worries if not".
- **R1 Honesty.** "3 clubs in your league have already signed up" needs a source the writer can show. Fix: check the
  number and say which clubs may be named, or cut it.
- **R6 Consent.** Nothing says the club asked to hear about hire. Fix: state the basis for writing to them.
- **R4 Pronoun stance.** "our new scoreboard hire" for a one-person business. Fix: "my".

## 3. A membership-page paragraph (kind: page)

> Join today and unlock endless savings! Members pay $40 a year. Terms apply. Spots are filling fast.

- **R3 Pressure.** "Spots are filling fast" is urgency with nothing behind it. Fix: state the real limit, if there
  is one, or cut it.
- **R12 Scope.** "Terms apply" hides what is and isn't included. Fix: list the two or three terms that matter in
  one line.
- **R1 Honesty.** "endless savings" has no figure to support it. Fix: name one saving the writer can prove.
- **R5 Formality.** The exclamation reads chummier than a casual-professional register. Fix: a full stop.
