The Elm Street Community Garden has been going since 2019. There are 40 of us now, and we share seeds, tools and
advice, with a working bee most Saturdays. Our 12 raised beds grow herbs, vegetables and native plants. You don't need
to know what you're doing to join: plenty of us started out unable to tell a weed from a seedling, and someone will
always show you where the watering cans live.
