# Brief: a product update for a to-do app

Audience: people who already use a small to-do list app on their phone.

Format: an update post for the app's blog, with a short title and two or three short sections.

Length: about 300 words.

Topic: this month's release of a to-do app, with recurring tasks, sharing a list with one other person, and a fix
for reminders that arrived late.

Explain what changed, who it suits, and how to turn on recurring tasks. Mention one thing that didn't make this
release and when to expect it.

My notes (use only these facts):

- this is version 2.6, out 14 Oct on iPhone + Android. update from the app store as normal
- recurring tasks: daily, weekly, monthly, or "every X days". good for bins night, plant watering, paying rent
- to turn on: open the task > tap the little clock icon > Repeat > pick how often > Save. a loop symbol then shows next to it
- tick off a repeating task and the next one appears straight away w/ the new date
- sharing: one list w/ one other person. Lists > ... menu > Share > send the invite link. they need the app too
- both people can add, edit + tick things off, changes show on the other phone within a few seconds
- built it bc lots of you asked for a shared shopping list for couples + housemates
- late reminders fix: on some Android phones w/ battery saver on, reminders were turning up 10-20 min late. fixed in 2.6
- if reminders still run late after updating, email us from Settings > Help
- didn't make it: sharing a list w/ more than one person. still testing, aiming for the December release

Use only the facts in these notes, and don't add statistics or figures of your own.
