# Brief: learning a new skill

Audience: the writer's professional contacts on LinkedIn, mostly people in office jobs.

Format: a LinkedIn post in short paragraphs, with no hashtags in the body and at most three at the end.

Length: 150 to 200 words.

Topic: learning to touch-type properly over three months, after years of typing with two fingers.

Describe what was hard at the start, what changed, and one thing a reader could try this week if they want to pick up
a skill of their own.

My notes (use only these facts):

- typed w/ 2 fingers for ~15 yrs, all through uni + 3 office jobs
- started mid July, finished mid Oct. 15 min every morning before work on a free typing practice site
- start: 28 words/min two-finger
- wk 1 dropped to 11 wpm, felt ridiculous, nearly quit twice
- put a tea towel over my hands so I couldn't look down - worked better than willpower
- month 2: around 35 wpm, emails stopped feeling like a chore
- end of month 3: 52 wpm, 96% accuracy on the practice site
- numbers row + Q and Z still slow
- best change: I look at the screen not the keys, so I catch typos as I go + can keep eye contact on video calls while taking notes
- what I'd suggest: pick one small skill, same 15 min same time daily, write one number down each Friday so you can see it move

Use only the facts in these notes, and don't add statistics or figures of your own.
