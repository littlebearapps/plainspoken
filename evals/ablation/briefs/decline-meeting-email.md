# Brief: declining a meeting

Audience: a colleague in another team who has invited you to a one-hour planning meeting next week.

Format: a short email with a subject line, a greeting and a sign-off.

Length: 80 to 120 words for the email body.

Topic: politely declining a planning meeting because of a deadline that week.

Ask them to send the notes afterwards, or propose a 15-minute call the following week. Keep the tone friendly and
direct.

My notes (use only these facts):

- colleague = Priya, events team. me = Sam, finance team
- her invite: summer events planning, Tue 21 Oct, 10-11am, level 3 meeting room
- my clash: quarterly budget report due to the board Thu 23 Oct 5pm, whole week is heads-down on it
- I'd still like to hear what's decided, esp anything that changes the events budget for Jan-Mar
- notes are fine, or a 15 min call - I'm free Mon 27 Oct after 2pm or Tue 28 Oct any time before 11
- happy for her to just send the call invite if one of those works
- sign off with first name only

Use only the facts in these notes, and don't add statistics or figures of your own.
