# Brief: what a domain name is

Audience: a small business owner setting up a website for the first time, with no technical background.

Format: a plain explainer for a help page, in short paragraphs with one sample web address.

Length: about 250 words.

Topic: what a domain name is, how it differs from a website and from web hosting, roughly what one costs each
year, and what happens when the owner forgets to renew it.

Use everyday comparisons and define any technical word the first time it appears.

My notes (use only these facts):

- this is for the help page on my small web design business's site. sample address to use: www.example.com
- how I explain it to clients: domain = your street address, website = the house, hosting = the block of land the house
  sits on, which you rent
- registrar = the company you pay to hold the name for you. hosting = the computer that stores your site's files and
  shows them to visitors
- what our clients pay (we pass it on at cost): .com.au about $25/yr, .com about $20/yr
- hosting is a separate bill on our plans, $15/month
- you don't buy a domain forever, you rent it a year (or a few) at a time
- our registrar gives 30 days after expiry to renew at the normal price. after that the name can be released and anyone
  can buy it
- real one from March: a client's card expired so auto-renew failed. site + email stopped working the same day
- she renewed on day 12, paid the normal fee, but lost nearly 2 wks of customer emails
- my tip: turn on auto-renew AND keep the card on file up to date

Use only the facts in these notes, and don't add statistics or figures of your own.
