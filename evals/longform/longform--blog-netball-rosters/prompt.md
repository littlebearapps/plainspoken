Write a blog post (about 500 words) for our club website about how we moved our rosters online.

> - Club: Brunswick Netball Club, 14 teams, about 160 players, juniors from under-9s to open women's.
> - Until this season, rosters went out on a printed sheet handed out at training and a message in a 140-person group chat.
> - Late changes (a court swap, a wet-weather call) often didn't reach families: the sheet was already printed and the chat message got buried.
> - In February we moved to a free shared calendar that families subscribe to on their phones; each team has its own calendar.
> - Two volunteers, Kath Nguyen and Ollie Ward, set it up over three weekends, entering every fixture by hand.
> - Kath said: 'The first weekend was all copying fixtures. The second was fixing the ones I'd copied wrong.'
> - Missed-game messages to the coordinator dropped from about 20 a week to about 5.
> - Problem: some older families and grandparents who drive kids found subscribing hard, especially on older phones.
> - Fix: a 15-minute help session after Thursday training in round 3; about a dozen families came.
> - We still print a sheet for the noticeboard at the courts.
> - Next: putting the canteen roster in the same calendars, starting next season.
> - Written by the club secretary, Jo Ferris, first person, warm but not gushing.
