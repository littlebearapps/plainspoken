# Brief: learning a new skill

Audience: the writer's professional contacts on LinkedIn, mostly people in office jobs.

Format: a LinkedIn post in short paragraphs, with no hashtags in the body and at most three at the end.

Length: 150 to 200 words.

Topic: learning to touch-type properly over three months, after years of typing with two fingers.

Describe what was hard at the start, what changed, and one thing a reader could try this week if they want to pick up
a skill of their own.
