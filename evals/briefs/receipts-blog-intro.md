# Brief: keeping receipts for tax time

Audience: people who run a side business or work for themselves and dread the end of the financial year.

Format: the opening of a blog post, in plain paragraphs with no headings or lists.

Length: about 500 words.

Topic: holding on to receipts through the year, so tax returns are less of a scramble at the end of the financial
year.

Cover what to file, a simple way to store paper and emailed receipts, and how long to hold on to them. Write in the
first person, as someone who learnt this the hard way.
