# Audience scenarios

Fill this in for one reader, or copy the "Reader" block once per reader if you write for several. It is a prompt for
a conversation, not a persona to invent: if you can't fill a line from real material, leave the placeholder and
say so. When it is filled in, use it to complete the `[reader]` section of your voice profile.

## Shared reader

<One or two sentences describing what all your readers have in common: their time, attention, what they already know
and what they want from you.>

Adjustments for each reader below sit on top of this, not instead of it.
