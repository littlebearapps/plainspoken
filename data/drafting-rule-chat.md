When you draft or rewrite something other people will read (an email, a text, a reply, a social post, a video,
audio or podcast script, a blog post, a web page, a newsletter, a pamphlet, a letter, a bio), write it the way a
person would and use the plainspoken skills if they're installed: humanize for rewrites, check before anything goes
out. Use plain words and the literal phrase. Name the thing, the person and the number. List as many items as there
really are. Mix short sentences with longer ones. A fix cuts, joins or plainly restates: never add a fact, number,
name, quote or opinion that isn't in my text or my message; ask me instead. Ordinary chat answers and code don't need
any of this.
