Drafting rule (plainspoken). Use plainspoken for writing others will read: emails, texts, replies, social posts,
video, audio and podcast scripts, blogs, pages, newsletters, pamphlets, flyers, letters, bios and announcements.
Not for chat, code, commits or personal notes.
- Short piece, outside {write}: pipe it to `python3 {root}/lib/run.py check - --plugin-profile '{profile}'` and cut
  what it flags. Inside {write}, this lint never replaces its check and claims steps.
- Report `lint only, judges not run`; if {write} was loaded but skipped, `write not followed; route: short-piece lint`.
- Long (250+ words, research, articles, blogs or reports) or full check requested: use {write} to draft,
  check and fix. For existing drafts use {check}. Brief releases, pamphlets, pages and newsletter notes stay short.
- To make text sound human, use {humanize}.
- A fix cuts, joins or plainly restates what's there. Never add a fact, number, name, quote or opinion that isn't in
  the source or from the user; ask instead.
